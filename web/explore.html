03 / The company

Who shared the bill?

Each circle is a listed opening act. Its area reflects how often it appeared with Fugazi. A line joins two acts listed at the same Fugazi show; thicker lines mean more shared bills.

Select an act to highlight its connections and see its shows. On narrow screens, scroll the network sideways or use the act names below. Position is for readability; distance does not measure musical similarity. A shared bill does not imply a collaboration.

Reading the archive

This is an independent fan project, unaffiliated with Fugazi or Dischord Records. Missing setlists and opening-act lists are unknown, not zeros. Counts describe the published metadata and change with the filters above. Track labels awaiting review remain available in Search.

The song ranking includes release-matched catalog entries, omitting unresolved labels such as intros and interludes. The opener network retains archive identities and excludes the unidentified “?” label. Country names remain as published; the map uses current city coordinates, including for historical labels.
