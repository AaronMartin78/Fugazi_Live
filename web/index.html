About this data

This is an independent fan project, unaffiliated with Fugazi or Dischord Records. The source is the public Fugazi Live Series archive. Support the archive by visiting Dischord; recordings and purchases stay there.

Missing setlists are unknown, not evidence that a song was not played. Track labels include intros, interludes and variants. Counts describe published metadata; repeated entries are preserved. Country labels are retained as published.
